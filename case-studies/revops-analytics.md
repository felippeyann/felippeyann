# RevOps data platform for a financial-market consultancy

**Context**: a RevOps consultancy serving firms in the Brazilian financial market. I designed and operate the data layer behind its commercial operation. Company and clients are anonymized; no client data appears here.

**My role**: data platform architecture, pipelines, LLM evaluation systems, analytics and data hygiene, working alongside the leadership team.

## The platform

- **Operational data store (ODS)** on Supabase (Postgres): consolidates the CRM, the marketing automation platform, call/meeting recordings metadata and enrichment sources into one queryable model, with migrations applied through PR-gated CI (a direct push never touches the schema).
- **Sync health as a first-class concern**: each integration has diagnostic queries and documented failure modes, including the known false alarms, so an on-call check distinguishes "pipeline broken" from "quiet weekend" in minutes.
- **Auditable reporting**: monthly RevOps panels generated by SQL RPCs inside the product, plus standalone HTML reports published to storage with signed URLs, so every number leadership sees links back to its data snapshot.

## LLM evaluation engine (in production)

An evaluation engine for sales meetings and calls, handed over to the company's CTO:

- **Two-call architecture**: the first LLM call extracts structured evidence from the transcript; the second grades against a rubric; a deterministic `computeScore` turns rubric grades into the final score, so scoring is reproducible and the LLM never does arithmetic.
- Calibration sets and methodology-drift checks are part of the design: the failure mode of "the rubric quietly changed meaning over months" is treated as a real risk, not an afterthought.

## Data hygiene, the unglamorous part that pays

- **Field retirement protocol**: before deleting a legacy CRM field, snapshot its history to the ODS, patch every writer, then delete. No silent data loss.
- **Phone/contact anomaly detection**: found and suppressed a class of corporate phone numbers stamped onto dozens of individual profiles (which polluted dedup and outreach), with blocklist thresholds tuned to catch it.
- **Performance work**: migrated the analytics front-end from Chart.js to ECharts with deferred/lazy loading after profiling real usage.

## What this project demonstrates

Building a revenue-operations data platform that people actually trust: reproducible LLM scoring, health-checked syncs, auditable reports, and disciplined schema evolution in a live business.
